Questions, answered

White label casino software FAQs

What is a white label casino platform?

A white label casino platform is a ready-built online casino solution provided by a third-party vendor. The operator brands it as their own and focuses on marketing. The vendor handles the technology, game integration, payment processing, and often licensing. It is the fastest and most cost-effective route to launching a legal online casino.

How much does it cost to launch a white label casino?

Setup costs range from $15,000 (entry-level rapid deployment) to $150,000+ (enterprise platforms). The ongoing cost is typically a monthly fee ($2,000 to $15,000) plus a GGR revenue share of 8 to 15%. Total first-year cost for a mid-range white label launch is typically $60,000 to $150,000 before marketing.

How long does it take to launch a white label casino?

The fastest providers (NuxGame, Oddsgate) can have you operational in 24 to 72 hours using a pre-built template. A full white label deployment with custom branding and integrations typically takes 2 to 8 weeks. Enterprise platforms can take 3 to 6 months.

Do I need my own gambling licence?

Not necessarily. Several providers on this list offer sublicensing, meaning operators launch under the provider's existing Curaçao or MGA licence. This is faster and cheaper than obtaining your own. For long-term operations in regulated markets, your own licence is the better path.

What is the difference between white label and turnkey?

With a white label, the provider retains ownership of the platform and licence. You brand it and market it. With a turnkey solution, you receive the full platform code and typically own it outright after setup. That gives you more control, but also more responsibility for compliance and maintenance.

Which white label casino platform is best for crypto?

SOFTSWISS and NuxGame are the strongest crypto-native platforms on this list. SOFTSWISS has deeper infrastructure and more established operator deployments. NuxGame has a Web3 API and lower entry cost, making it better suited to crypto-first startups.
