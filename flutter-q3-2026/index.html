The quarter in numbers

Revenue up, profit gone

Flutter made $3.87 billion of revenue, up 2% from $3.79 billion a year earlier and below the roughly $4.03 billion analysts expected. Its net loss grew from $789 million to $1.15 billion, and adjusted EBITDA, the operating profit measure Flutter uses to guide investors, fell 25% to $360 million.

Two segments, one direction

Profit fell in both halves of the business

Flutter reports two segments: the US, which is mostly FanDuel, and International, which covers everything else. Adjusted EBITDA fell in both. International revenue was flat at $2.42 billion and monthly players fell 8% to 9.8 million. In the US, revenue grew 6% to $1.46 billion, but almost none of it reached the bottom line.

The Brazil write-off

What happened in Brazil

Brazil banned online sports betting and casino games with an emergency measure on 25 September 2026, and Flutter closed its Brazilian operations the next day. The result was an $873 million one-off, non-cash write-off. Flutter owns 56% of its Brazilian business, and its own share of the charge is $537 million. It is closing its Betfair Brazil brand and keeping Betnacional as its only brand there.

The share price

Down 64% in a year

Flutter's share price stood at $229.85 on 10 November 2025, and on 9 November 2026 it closed at $81.99. The steepest fall came in January and February 2026, when the price roughly halved, and it slid again in late September after the Brazil ban. Google Finance puts Flutter's market value at about $14.3 billion on the New York Stock Exchange. A position of $10,000 held a year ago would be worth about $3,567 now; the price would need to rise 180% to get back.

Outlook

Guidance cut, buybacks paused

Flutter lowered its 2026 forecast across the board and paused its share buyback programme to focus on paying down debt, after buying back about $250 million of its own shares earlier in 2026. Net debt stood at $10.7 billion at the end of September, 4.6 times adjusted EBITDA, up from 3.7 times at the end of 2025. Flutter extended the maturity of part of its debt from 2028 to 2032.

Questions, answered

Flutter Q3 2026 FAQs

Why did Flutter report a loss in Q3 2026?

Mainly because of an $873 million non-cash write-off after Brazil banned online betting in September 2026. Flutter's net loss was $1.15 billion.

How did FanDuel perform in Q3 2026?

US revenue grew 6% to $1.46 billion and monthly players rose 12% to 3.9 million, but US adjusted EBITDA fell 94% to $3 million.

Did Flutter cut its guidance?

Yes. Its 2026 revenue forecast fell by $300 million to $17.61 billion, and adjusted EBITDA by $160 million to $2.50 billion, at the midpoints.

Why did Flutter leave Brazil?

Brazil issued an emergency measure on 25 September 2026 banning online sports betting and casino games. Flutter closed its operations there on 26 September.

How much has Flutter stock fallen?

About 64% in a year, from $229.85 on 10 November 2025 to $81.99 on 9 November 2026.
